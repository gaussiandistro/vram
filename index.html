About VRAM

  • VRAM is a student developed project built for the LHWHS community.
  • Jiaxiang Xie: Creator and developer of VRAM
  • Ian Rodrigues: Developer and contributor
  • VRAM is actively maintained and updated by both developers.
  • For those who are just here to check the time, we plan to keep VRAM minimalistic and user friendly :>
  • Thanks to everyone who submits feedback, bug reports, and feature ideas! Requests for features unrelated to school will be ignored and thrown down the stairs.

Changelog

UI Polish

September 19, 2026

  • The countdown and progress ring now glow red and pulse during the last minute of a period.
  • The progress ring now fills smoothly instead of jumping every second.
  • Added a loading shimmer while the schedule loads.
  • The theme customizer now warns you when your text and background colors are hard to read (WCAG contrast check).

Theme Customizer!

September 18, 2026

  • This was a heavily requested feature, and it's finally here!
  • Any font from fonts.google.com can be used as a custom font for VRAM! Some fonts may break the formatting, please use at your own discretion.
  • The background color and the text color can be customized with and RGB slider or a hex input.
  • Any changes can be reset using the respective buttons.
  • The notifications panel now shows up on mobile.
